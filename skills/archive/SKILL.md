---
name: archive
description: Personal knowledge archive (pull-based, explicit). Use ONLY when the user explicitly asks to archive, save, or store knowledge, or to look something up in their archive, OR when their question unambiguously refers to their own stored knowledge, prior decisions, preferences, or procedures (e.g. "what did I decide about X", "do I have notes on Y"). Never load the archive ambiently; never consult it for ordinary coding or research tasks.
---

Run `archive prompt` and follow the contract it prints exactly. It defines the ingest algorithm (search for prior art first, update the one authoritative entry on a strong match, distill instead of transcribing, curated categories, source pointers, raw stashing) and the retrieval rules (silent targeted search only, results used only when clearly on-topic).

If the `archive` binary is not on PATH, say so instead of improvising storage elsewhere.
